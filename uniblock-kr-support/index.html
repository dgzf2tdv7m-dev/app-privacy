Offline filter tools

UniBlock KR Support

UniBlock KR provides local Safari filtering rules, category controls, rule status, and export notes for Korean users.

Support contact: support@fengkit.com

Data

Filter preferences and exported notes stay on your device unless you share them through iOS system sharing.

Access

UniBlock KR works offline after installation. It does not include ads or accounts.

Independence

This is an independent utility and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or certified by any browser or advertising platform.
