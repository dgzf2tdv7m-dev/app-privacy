PlanGrid KR Support

PlanGrid KR is a daily planner for 10-minute schedule blocks, reusable Korean schedule templates, weekly statistics, focus timer records, and local export.

Contact

Email: support@fengkit.com

Common Help

  • Use the Templates tab to start from one of 15 ready-made routines.
  • Use the weekly statistics panel to review completed blocks, focus time, and postponed items.
  • Export creates local CSV/PDF-style summaries on device.

Privacy

No account is required. Planner content stays on device unless you choose to export or share it.
