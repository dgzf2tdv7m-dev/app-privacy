Offline astronomy planner

CosmiSky KR Support

CosmiSky KR keeps sky notes, viewing checklists, local exports and planning records on the device. It does not require an account or cloud sync.

Help

If the app does not respond on iPad, close and reopen the app after installing the latest build. All primary controls are designed with touch targets suitable for iPad and iPhone.
